# Close The Sandbox Through Its Offline Fallback

Status: complete. Hosted work stopped after the accepted offline evidence; KAP-0073 later removed
the deployable implementation.

Historical owners: [KAP-0069](KAP-0069.md), [KAP-0072](KAP-0072.md),
[sandbox API](../docs/SANDBOX_API.md), and [sandbox deployment](../docs/SANDBOX_DEPLOYMENT.md).

## Preserved evidence

The unpublished offline composition proved:

- fixed v1 admission and projection fixtures over the root `Application`;
- one-active capacity through cleanup absence;
- separate native runner handoff and same-operation recovery;
- descriptor transfer, OS identity, cgroup fencing, and hostile-parent hardening;
- fixed synthetic-cluster policy and bounded cleanup authority;
- authority staging, dispatch pins, retained trust, and reference-safe collection; and
- deletion of the superseded backup/restore branch while retained gates stayed green.

Exact backup/restore checkpoint `bde1e3ba72058d2ca5787fbe8cc018cc8ced1da2` remains at
`archive/kap-0070-backup-restore-bde1e3b`. It is historical evidence, not a deployment route.

## Fallback

The fallback retained fixed fixtures plus the release-owned real-process and disposable-cluster
demonstrations. It did not continue hosted failure-domain composition, sandbox reopen, abuse-edge
control, provider inventory, host bundling, or public exposure.

KAP-0073 then archived the final narrowed source and removed `crates/kapsel-sandbox`,
`deploy/sandbox`, sandbox-only scripts, workspace wiring, and active deployment claims.

## Acceptance

- No live sandbox gate remains.
- Historical fixtures and archive identities remain discoverable.
- Root lifecycle, receiver-result, `UNKNOWN`, receipt, CLI/MCP, and local demonstration behavior are
  unchanged.
- Removed sandbox surfaces are not current architecture or compatibility contracts.

## Residual risk

The archived sandbox never passed complete hosted isolation, recovery, cleanup, or public-exposure
qualification. It must not be reconstructed or reused as a resident-service template.
