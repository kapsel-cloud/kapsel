## Why this is complete rather than failed

The sandbox was a comprehension surface, not the customer-resident product. Its remaining
requirements were created by accepting anonymous public traffic: edge abuse authority, synthetic
infrastructure, per-run isolation, cleanup inventory, independent traffic cutoff, and catastrophic
recreation. They do not establish protected customer-resident authority, installability, workflow
integration, or retained use.

The fallback preserves the exact mechanism proof without continuing a second hosted system. Product
risk now lies in whether another team installs and retains the root mechanism, not whether a visitor
can watch it through a browser.
