## Acceptance

- Both fixed scenarios reach classifier-complete receipts through the public interface.
- Browser or client disconnect and runner restart recover the same operation without a blind second
  mutation.

  independently observable from operation classification.

  revision.

## Residual risk

All live properties remain unproved. Authority Composition's normalized patch model is not evidence
that a real Kubernetes admission implementation enforces the rule, and its static-volume/backup
composition is not evidence of storage-class support, snapshot atomicity, primary fencing, restore
exclusion, or managed key custody. The native process has no public-traffic approval. Its periodic
retention role now exists and is represented in an incomplete, non-applicable evidence wrapper but
is not deployable or deployed; scheduler and cleanup-controller composition plus the accepted
runner's provider binding and deployment remain unimplemented. Infrastructure Enforcement Proof must
compose those operations under the selected workload, key, and storage identities. A public
demonstration remains an abuse and operating-cost surface; namespace policy may be insufficient
against a compromised workload or cluster-level defect, managed sandbox compatibility and key
custody may require interface review, cleanup may outlive the experiment, and fixed infrastructure
costs may exceed a future approved ceiling.
