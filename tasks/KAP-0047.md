# Record v0.2.0 Artifact Defects

Status: available for reproducible technical findings against the exact published developer-beta
artifact.

Baseline:

- version: `v0.2.0`;
- source revision: `5fe0407cd5ebd78f386905e33f79e0f712a57835`;
- archive SHA-256: `82db38b05772b1b6a4ef9a9312baf9633f0ce652ddfe8052ed7df86d1fd1d969`;
- supported target: `x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu`.

Owners: [technical scope](../docs/SCOPE.md), [v0.2 release design](../docs/V0.2.md),
[evaluator guide](../docs/EVALUATOR.md), [testing strategy](../docs/TESTING.md), and
[privacy boundary](../docs/PRIVACY.md).

## Record only reproducible technical facts

A report must identify the exact artifact and environment, provide the smallest reproduction, state
expected and observed behavior, and classify the affected owner:

- correctness or receiver-result semantics;
- security or authority boundary;
- documentation;
- packaging or installation;
- compatibility or migration; or
- supported-platform behavior.

Separate defects from feature requests. Open a finite correction task before changing released
behavior; published tags and assets remain immutable. Security findings follow `SECURITY.md` before
public disclosure.

Do not record identities, private operational data, credentials, or consequential workloads.
Requests for another operation do not change the sole-capability scope.

## Acceptance for a finding

- Exact release identity and supported environment are present.
- Reproduction uses no production credentials or consequential cluster.
- Logs and receipts are bounded and scrubbed under `docs/PRIVACY.md`.
- The direct technical owner is named.
- The report states whether the finding reproduces and what remains unproved.
